The world globe uses Natural Earth 1:110m land geometry.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
License: Public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The dots and static globe illustration are generated with `scripts/generate-globe.mjs`.
The connection paths are decorative and do not represent cases, users, or service locations.
